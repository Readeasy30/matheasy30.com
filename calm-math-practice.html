The Calm Math Method

1

Read Slowly

Make sure the learner understands what the problem asks.

2

Try One Step

Do not solve everything at once. Start with the first small move.

3

Use a Hint

Hints are tools. They help learners keep going.

4

Review Calmly

Mistakes show what to practice next.

What to Avoid

  • Do not rush every answer.
  • Do not shame wrong answers.
  • Do not practice too many skills at once.
  • Do not keep going after overload.
