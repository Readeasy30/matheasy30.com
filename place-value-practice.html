Place Value Practice

Build numbers with ones, tens, and hundreds.

Place value helps learners understand what each digit means.

Simple place value steps

  • Look at each digit.
  • Find its place.
  • Say its value.
  • Build the full number.
Open MathEasy30

Example

347

3 hundreds, 4 tens, and 7 ones.

That means 300 + 40 + 7.

Helper tip

Use blocks, sticks, or drawings for hundreds, tens, and ones. Learners should see the number, not just say it.
