Printable Math Worksheets

Simple math practice you can read, write, copy, or print.

Use these calm worksheet ideas with MathEasy30 lessons. Keep the numbers small, read directions slowly, and praise careful thinking instead of speed.

Open Math App Addition Help

How to use these worksheets

Pick one small skill. Practice for 10 to 20 minutes. Stop before the learner feels defeated. MathEasy30 works best when practice feels safe and steady.

  • Read each question out loud if needed.
  • Use fingers, coins, blocks, paper, or drawings.
  • Ask the learner to explain one step.
  • Circle problems that need more practice later.

🫧 Bubbles Says

One clear worksheet is better than ten rushed pages. Slow practice builds real confidence.

Worksheet starter set

Counting worksheet

Write numbers 1 to 20. Circle the numbers that come after 4, 9, 13, and 18.

Addition worksheet

Solve: 2 + 1, 3 + 2, 4 + 4, 6 + 3, 8 + 2. Draw dots if needed.

Subtraction worksheet

Solve: 5 - 1, 7 - 2, 10 - 5, 9 - 3, 12 - 4. Cross out dots to show taking away.

Word problem worksheet

Mia has 4 apples. She gets 3 more. How many apples does she have now? Draw the story.

Math facts worksheet

Practice five facts slowly. Say the pattern before writing the answer.

Fractions worksheet

Draw one circle. Split it into 2 equal parts. Shade one half. Then try fourths.
