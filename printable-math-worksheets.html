Start With One Page

Do not print everything at once. Choose one short worksheet and help the learner explain one step clearly.

  • Read each question out loud if needed.
  • Use fingers, coins, blocks, paper, or drawings.
  • Ask the learner to explain one step.
  • Praise careful thinking instead of speed.
  • Stop before practice feels stressful.

🫧 Bubbles Says

One clear worksheet is better than ten rushed pages. Slow practice builds real confidence.

Fractions Worksheet

Practice equal parts, halves, fourths, fraction words, simple drawings, and helper explanations.

  • Draw one circle. Split it into 2 equal parts. Shade one half.
  • Draw one rectangle. Split it into 4 equal parts. Shade one fourth.
  • Explain how you know the parts are equal.
