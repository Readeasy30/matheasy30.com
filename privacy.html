Privacy Policy

Privacy Matters

MathEasy30 is designed to stay simple, free, and learner-friendly.

Current Privacy Approach

MathEasy30 is a free practice website. The app may save simple progress on the same device using browser storage so the learner can continue later.

MathEasy30 does not currently require account sign-up, payment information, student names, or a public student profile.

Device Progress

Progress saved in the browser usually stays on that device and browser. Clearing browser data may reset progress.

Information You Choose to Send

If you contact MathEasy30, the information you send may be used to respond to your message or improve the site.

Please do not send private student records, passwords, school account details, or sensitive personal information.

Future Changes

If MathEasy30 adds new tools that change how information is handled, this page should be updated before those tools go live.

Contact

Questions may be sent through the current MathEasy30 project contact method.
