Privacy Policy

Privacy matters.

MathEasy30 is designed to stay simple and learner-friendly.

Current privacy approach

MathEasy30 is a simple practice website. The lesson app may save progress on the same device using browser storage so the learner can continue later.

MathEasy30 does not currently require account sign-up, payment information, or a student profile.

Information you choose to send

If you contact MathEasy30 by email, the information you send may be used to respond to your message or improve the site.

Future changes

If MathEasy30 adds accounts, analytics, subscriptions, ads, or other tools later, this page should be updated before those tools go live.

Contact

Questions may be sent to wholelychit@gmail.com.
