Money Math Practice

Practice money math for real life.

Coins, bills, prices, and change help learners see why math matters.

Simple money steps

  • Name each coin or bill.
  • Count from largest value to smallest.
  • Write dollars and cents clearly.
  • Check the total one more time.
Open MathEasy30

Example

25¢ + 10¢ + 5¢ = 40¢.

Counting coins slowly helps avoid guessing.

Helper tip

Use real or play coins. Money practice works best when learners can touch and sort the coins.
