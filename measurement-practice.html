Measurement Practice

Measure real things one step at a time.

Measurement connects math to daily life: length, weight, time, and size.

Simple measurement steps

  • Choose what you are measuring.
  • Pick the right tool.
  • Start at zero.
  • Read the number carefully.
  • Write the unit.
