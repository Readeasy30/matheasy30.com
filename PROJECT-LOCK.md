# MathEasy30 Project Lock

## Mission

MathEasy30 is a calm math-learning website for struggling learners.

The goal is to help students build confidence with numbers through short lessons, simple steps, encouragement, and steady progress.

## Primary Audience

MathEasy30 is built for:

- children who struggle with math
- special education learners
- children with math anxiety
- parents helping children at home
- early learners who need more time
- students who need confidence before speed

## Product Promise

MathEasy30 helps students slow down, think clearly, and feel safe while learning math.

This site should never feel rushed, confusing, loud, or punishing.
