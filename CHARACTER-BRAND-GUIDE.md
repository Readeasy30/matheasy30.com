# Character Brand Guide — MathEasy30

Last updated: 2026-06-01

## Official Math Mascot

**Matthew Math™** is the official math mascot for MathEasy30.

## Locked Character Direction

Matthew Math™ is:

- friendly
- encouraging
- calm
- smart without feeling intimidating
- patient with struggling math learners
- clear and step-by-step
- safe for children, adults, ESL learners, tutors, parents, libraries, adult education groups, and workforce programs

## Locked Visual Direction

Matthew Math™ should usually appear with:

- curly dark hair
- blue hoodie or blue learning outfit
- math-friendly school look
- warm smile
- ruler, pencil, notebook, number blocks, or shape props
- clear, family-friendly design

## Brand Role

Matthew Math™ is not an open-ended chatbot.

He is a calm math guide who helps learners feel:

- safe
- unjudged
- patient
- ready to try one small step

## Approved Core Messages

Use these messages when appropriate:

- 100% free math practice.
- 30 minutes a day.
- Math practice without pressure.
- One calm step at a time.
- Think. Solve. Grow.

## Required Website Address

Always use the complete .com address in marketing materials:

- `https://matheasy30.com/`

When cross-promoting ReadEasy30, also include:

- `https://readeasy30.com/`

## Where Matthew Math™ Should Appear

Use Matthew Math™ on:

- homepage hero or welcome section
- math app welcome area
- printable math worksheets
- parent/helper math guide
- adult math practice page
- Pinterest pins
- YouTube thumbnails
- short video intros
- outreach handouts
- library/adult education flyers
- workforce/basic skills materials

## Where To Avoid Overusing Him

Do not place Matthew on every section if it makes the page feel cluttered.

Best rule:

- use one strong hero image
- use small expression icons for tips or encouragement
- use worksheet/footer images sparingly

## Trademark / IP Note

Use the mark as:

**Matthew Math™**

Do not use the registered trademark symbol unless registration is completed.
