# MathEasy30 Days 1-240 Master Curriculum

Complete source roadmap before app wiring. This file keeps the full math path in one place.

## Product promise

- 30 minutes a day.
- 8 levels of 30 days.
- 240 total practice days.
- Goal: build toward 8th-grade math readiness.
- Honest wording: progress depends on starting level, consistency, support, memory, attention, and confidence.
- Repeat is allowed. Repeating a level is practice, not failure.
