# ReadEasy30 + MathEasy30 Build Brief

Last updated: 2026-05-28

## Mission

ReadEasy30 and MathEasy30 are free, calm, no-shame practice websites that help adults, students, ESL learners, and learners who need extra support build reading and math confidence toward about an 8th-grade level.

The sites should feel respectful for adults and older learners while still being simple enough for children, parents, tutors, and helpers.

## Core Promise

- Practice for 30 minutes a day.
- Go slowly.
- Build skill.
- Build confidence.
- No shame.
- No pressure.
- No complicated setup.

## Audience

These sites serve:

- adults improving reading or math
- ESL learners
- special education learners
- students behind grade level
- homeschool families
- parents helping children
- tutors and aides
- older learners
- anyone who wants extra practice

## Public Language

Use:

> Free practice for adults, students, ESL learners, and learners who need extra support.

Use:

> Build skills toward about an 8th-grade level with calm daily practice.

Avoid:

- babyish wording
- shame language
- guaranteed improvement claims
- medical or diagnostic claims
- school replacement claims

## Product Standard

Each site must be:

- free at launch
- mobile-first
- fast
- simple
- respectful
- no account required at first
- no payment required at first
- no tracking at first
- no ads at first
- no public AI at first
- static HTML/CSS/JS unless already using another stack

## Learning Standard

Each lesson should include:

1. Simple goal
2. Short teaching section
3. Example
4. Guided practice
5. Independent practice
6. Calm feedback
7. Progress saved locally
8. Helper note for parent, tutor, adult supporter, or aide

## No-Shame Design Rules

Do not say:

- Failed
- Bad score
- Wrong again
- You should know this
- Too easy for you
- Too hard for you

Use:

- Try again
- Good practice
- Let's slow down
- Look back at the example
- This is a good place to practice
- Mistakes show what to work on next

## Shared Level System

Both sites should use Level A through Level H.

- Level A: early beginner
- Level B: beginner
- Level C: early elementary
- Level D: elementary
- Level E: upper elementary
- Level F: early middle school
- Level G: middle school
- Level H: about 8th-grade level

Placement results should say "Start Here," not "You failed."

## Shared Daily Practice Structure

A 30-minute practice session should include:

1. Warm-up: 3 to 5 minutes
2. Lesson: 8 to 10 minutes
3. Guided practice: 8 to 10 minutes
4. Independent practice: 5 to 7 minutes
5. Review and encouragement: 2 to 3 minutes

## Coach Voice

The coach voice should be calm, patient, clear, respectful, and never babyish.

Example messages:

- Go slowly. One step at a time.
- Look back at the example.
- You are practicing, not racing.
- Mistakes help us know what to practice next.

## ReadEasy30 Focus

ReadEasy30 teaches reading confidence, everyday vocabulary, paragraph reading, comprehension, and reading for daily life toward about an 8th-grade level.

## MathEasy30 Focus

MathEasy30 teaches number sense, operations, fractions, decimals, percentages, money math, measurement, word problems, charts, geometry basics, ratios, and simple equations toward about an 8th-grade level.

## Monetization Later

The sites stay free.

Possible later money paths:

- printable workbook PDFs
- optional premium practice packs
- parent/tutor resource bundles
- education-friendly sponsorships
- carefully selected affiliate links to books/tools with disclosure
- ads only after privacy and layout review

Do not add monetization until the learning product is strong.

## Safety and Trust

Do not claim:

- guaranteed grade improvement
- disability treatment
- diagnosis
- certified tutoring unless true
- school replacement
- guaranteed reading or math level by a certain date

Use:

- extra practice
- confidence-building
- skill-building
- not a replacement for school, professional tutoring, IEP support, or specialist support

## Locked Direction

ReadEasy30 and MathEasy30 are calm, free, respectful learning websites for people who need practice without shame.

Build once. Keep it simple. Make it useful.
