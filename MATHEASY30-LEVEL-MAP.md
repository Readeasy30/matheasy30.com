# MathEasy30 Level Map

Last updated: 2026-05-28

## Goal

MathEasy30 helps learners build math confidence from beginner skills toward about an 8th-grade level.

The site should teach slowly, clearly, and respectfully for adults, students, ESL learners, and learners who need extra support.

## Level A — Early Beginner

Focus:

- numbers 0 to 20
- counting forward
- counting backward
- number matching
- more and less
- simple shapes
- simple patterns

Learner can practice:

- count objects
- choose the bigger number
- fill in a missing number
- name circle, square, triangle, rectangle
- finish a simple pattern

Example lesson:

> Count the apples. There are 6 apples.

## Level B — Beginner

Focus:

- numbers to 100
- place value basics
- addition within 20
- subtraction within 20
- number lines
- simple word problems

Learner can practice:

- 8 + 5
- 14 - 6
- count by 2s, 5s, and 10s
- compare 42 and 24
- solve one-step story problems

Example lesson:

> Tom has 7 pencils. He gets 3 more. 7 + 3 = 10.

## Level C — Early Elementary

Focus:

- addition with larger numbers
- subtraction with larger numbers
- place value to hundreds
- skip counting
- beginning multiplication idea
- time to hour and half hour
- money basics

Learner can practice:

- 34 + 25
- 70 - 28
- hundreds, tens, and ones
- groups of equal size
- count coins
- read simple clocks

Example lesson:

> 3 groups of 4 means 4 + 4 + 4.

## Level D — Elementary

Focus:

- multiplication facts
- division basics
- two-step word problems
- measurement
- time
- money math
- simple fractions

Learner can practice:

- 6 x 7
- 36 divided by 6
- 1/2 and 1/4
- inches, feet, cups, pounds
- elapsed time
- money change

Example lesson:

> A pizza is cut into 4 equal pieces. One piece is 1/4.

## Level E — Upper Elementary

Focus:

- multi-digit multiplication
- long division basics
- fractions
- decimals
- rounding
- perimeter
- area
- charts and tables

Learner can practice:

- 24 x 13
- 96 divided by 4
- 3/4 + 1/4
- decimals to hundredths
- round to nearest 10 or 100
- read a simple bar graph

Example lesson:

> Area means the space inside a shape.

## Level F — Early Middle School

Focus:

- fractions and decimals together
- percentages
- ratios
- rates
- coordinate grid
- basic geometry
- word problem strategy

Learner can practice:

- convert 1/2 to 0.5
- find 10% of 80
- compare 2:3 and 4:6
- plot points
- find a missing angle
- choose the operation in a word problem

Example lesson:

> Percent means out of 100.

## Level G — Middle School

Focus:

- integers
- expressions
- simple equations
- proportions
- unit rates
- data
- probability basics
- geometry formulas

Learner can practice:

- -3 + 8
- 2x + 5
- x + 7 = 12
- solve proportions
- miles per hour
- mean, median, mode
- area of triangles

Example lesson:

> An equation is like a balance. Both sides must stay equal.

## Level H — About 8th Grade

Focus:

- linear equations
- slope basics
- function idea
- scientific notation
- Pythagorean theorem basics
- systems idea
- real-world multi-step problems
- data interpretation

Learner can practice:

- 2x + 3 = 15
- find slope from a table
- understand y = mx + b
- use a graph
- solve percent increase and decrease
- basic Pythagorean theorem
- interpret charts

Example lesson:

> Slope tells how steep a line is.

## Lesson Pattern

Each MathEasy30 lesson should use this format:

1. Today's skill
2. Simple explanation
3. Worked example
4. Try it together
5. Practice problems
6. Word problem
7. Check answers
8. Coach message
9. Helper note

## Feedback Language

Use:

- Good practice.
- Try this step again.
- Look at the example.
- You are learning the pattern.
- Slow is fine.

Avoid:

- Wrong.
- Bad score.
- You failed.
