Word Problem Practice

Read the problem before solving.

Word problems get easier when learners slow down and find what the question is asking.

Simple word problem steps

  • Read the whole problem.
  • Circle the question.
  • Find the numbers.
  • Choose add, subtract, multiply, or divide.
  • Write the answer in a sentence.
Open MathEasy30

Example

Mia has 4 apples. Dad gives her 3 more. How many apples now?

4 + 3 = 7. Mia has 7 apples.

Helper tip

Ask: what happened in the story? Then ask: what do we need to find?
