Time Practice

Learn time with clocks and daily routines.

Time math helps learners plan the day, read clocks, and understand minutes and hours.

Simple time steps

  • Look at the hour hand.
  • Look at the minute hand.
  • Count minutes by fives when helpful.
  • Connect the time to a daily activity.
Open MathEasy30

Example

If the hour hand is near 3 and the minute hand is on 6, the time is 3:30.

That means half past 3.

Helper tip

Use a real clock and daily routines like meals, school, work, or bedtime.
