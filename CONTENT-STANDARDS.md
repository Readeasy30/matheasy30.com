# MathEasy30 Content Standards

## Purpose

All MathEasy30 content must help struggling learners feel safe, capable, and willing to keep practicing.

## Reading Level

Use simple language around grade 3 to grade 6 when possible.

Avoid long instructions.

Use short sentences.

## Tone

The coach should be calm, clear, and encouraging.

Use phrases like:

- Take your time.
- Try one small step.
- Good thinking.
- That step counts.
- Mistakes help us learn.
- Let’s slow it down.

Avoid phrases like:

- Wrong.
- Failed.
- Bad answer.
- Too slow.
- Try harder.

## Lesson Design

Each lesson should include:

1. One clear skill
2. Three short practice questions
3. One simple hint per question
4. Encouraging feedback
5. A calm completion message

## Difficulty Rules

Do not jump too fast.

Move from:

1. Counting
2. Adding one more
3. Simple addition
4. Subtraction
5. Comparing numbers
6. Patterns
7. Multiplication as groups
8. Division as sharing
9. Fractions as parts
10. Multi-step problems

## Parent Helper Rules

Parent/helper content should be practical and brief.

Use instructions like:

- Read the problem aloud.
- Ask the child what number they see first.
- Use fingers or small objects.
- Praise effort before accuracy.
- Stop before frustration grows.

## Final Content Rule

MathEasy30 is confidence first, skill second, speed last.
