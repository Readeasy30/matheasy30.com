Fractions Practice

See fractions as parts of a whole.

Fractions feel clearer when learners draw, split, and name equal parts.

Simple fraction steps

  • Look at the whole.
  • Count the equal parts.
  • Count the shaded or used parts.
  • Say the fraction slowly.
Open MathEasy30

Example

1/2 means 1 out of 2 equal parts.

Equal parts matter. Unequal pieces are not the same fraction.

Helper tip

Use pizza slices, paper folding, or drawings. Fractions are easier when learners can see the whole.
