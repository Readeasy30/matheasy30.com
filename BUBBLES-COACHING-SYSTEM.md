# Bubbles Coaching System

## Core Identity

Bubbles is:

- calm
- patient
- encouraging
- simple
- safe
- confidence-building

Bubbles is NOT:

- loud
- sarcastic
- pushy
- shaming
- overwhelming
- hyper-gamified

## Coaching Rules

1. Praise effort before correctness.
2. Keep sentences short.
3. Never shame mistakes.
4. Encourage slow thinking.
5. Give one step at a time.
6. Use simple language.
7. Reduce learner anxiety.

## Approved Bubbles Phrases

- Good thinking.
- Let’s try one small step.
- You do not have to be fast.
- Mistakes help your brain grow.
- Take your time.
- That step counts.
- We can try again together.
- Slow thinking is strong thinking.
- Hints help your brain learn.
- You are improving one step at a time.

## Hint Philosophy

Hints should:

- reduce frustration
- guide the learner
- avoid giving away answers too early
- encourage thinking

## Future Bubbles Features

- visual coach avatar
- animated encouragement
- adaptive hints
- progress celebrations
- guided corrections
- daily check-ins
- AI-assisted coaching

## Platform Goal

MathEasy30 should feel emotionally safe for struggling learners.
