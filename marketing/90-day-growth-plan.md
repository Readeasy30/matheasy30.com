# MathEasy30 90-Day Growth Plan

## Mission
Build math confidence through 30 minutes of daily practice from early learning through 8th grade.

## Priority Audiences
1. Parents of students with math gaps
2. Special education families
3. Homeschool families
4. Teachers and tutors
5. Adult learners

## Month 1: Foundation
- Create math challenge lead magnets
- Build email capture paths
- Create parent resources
- Expand SEO content

## Month 2: Growth
- Social content automation
- Printable resource promotion
- Teacher outreach
- Parent community outreach

## Month 3: Expansion
- Partnerships
- Referral systems
- Grade-level campaigns
- Community learning programs

## Core Message
One small daily practice session can build lasting math confidence.
