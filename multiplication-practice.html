Multiplication Practice

Use equal groups to multiply.

Multiplication is repeated groups. Slow thinking helps facts make sense.

Simple multiplication steps

  • Look for equal groups.
  • Count how many groups.
  • Count how many are in each group.
  • Add the groups or use a fact you know.
Open MathEasy30

Example

3 groups of 4

4 + 4 + 4 = 12, so 3 × 4 = 12.

Helper tip

Draw circles, rows, or arrays. Seeing equal groups helps multiplication feel clear.
