Parent Guide

Help without pressure.

MathEasy30 works best when the learner feels safe, calm, and unhurried.

How to help

  • Sit nearby, but let the learner try first.
  • Read the question aloud if needed.
  • Use fingers, blocks, coins, or paper for counting.
  • Praise effort before accuracy.
  • Stop before frustration grows too high.

Helpful phrases

  • Take your time.
  • Let’s try one small step.
  • Mistakes help your brain learn.
  • You do not have to be fast.
  • Good thinking.
